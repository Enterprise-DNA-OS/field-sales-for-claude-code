---
description: A rep week in the numbers a field manager asks for: calls, strike rate, average order, minutes in store, month against target.
---

1. `npm run field -- rep-week [<rep>]`. With no name it is the whole team side by side.
2. The four numbers that decide the conversation: calls completed, strike rate (the share of calls that took an order), average order value, and month to date against target. Lead with whichever one is furthest from where it should be.
3. Strike rate without call volume means nothing. A rep on 100 percent from one call is not ahead of a rep on 70 percent from ten. Say both.
4. Look at `no_order_reason` on the blank calls. If the same reason repeats across outlets, that is a range or a pricing problem, not a rep problem. Say so.
5. With a rep named, the command also prints the week call by call. Use it to answer "what did they actually do on Tuesday".
6. Close with the overdue calls from `/call-cycle` for that rep. That is next week's first job.

Do not rank reps whose territories are different sizes without saying so. Outlet count and drive time are in `/territory`.
